# Showcase asset notices

The four brand folders are synthetic examples. Their font files are redistributed only as part of those examples, with the following notices preserved alongside the fixtures:

- `orbit/assets/fonts/UbuntuSans*.ttf` — Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0, copyright Canonical Ltd. The licence text and current terms are published at https://ubuntu.com/legal/font-licence. The files are variable-font files; the regular and bold rendering is selected through the font weight axis.
- `parcelia/assets/fonts/LiberationSans*.ttf` — SIL Open Font License 1.1, copyright The Liberation Fonts Project. The licence text is published at https://openfontlicense.org and the project notice is at https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts/blob/main/LICENSE.
- `pyrus/assets/fonts/DejaVuSerif*.ttf` and `flux/assets/fonts/DejaVuSansMono*.ttf` — Bitstream Vera / DejaVu Fonts License, copyright the DejaVu Fonts contributors. The licence and redistribution conditions are published at https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html.

The image assets carry no third-party notice: `pyrus/assets/backgrounds/band-dark.png` and the other backgrounds and covers are generated procedurally for this repository, not photographs licensed from elsewhere.

These notices are part of the example distribution. A real brand that redistributes a font must carry the font's complete licence text and any required copyright notices with its own asset bundle; the report-baby renderer does not grant font licences.
